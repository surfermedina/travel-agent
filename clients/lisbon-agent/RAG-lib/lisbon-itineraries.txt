Sample 3-day Lisbon itinerary:

Day 1:
- Explore Baixa and Chiado
- Walk to Praça do Comércio
- Take Tram 28 through Alfama
- Visit a viewpoint like Miradouro de Santa Luzia

Day 2:
- Visit Belém Tower
- Tour Jerónimos Monastery
- Try pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém
- Walk along the waterfront

Day 3:
- Day trip to Sintra
- Visit Pena Palace and Quinta da Regaleira
- Return to Lisbon for dinner in Bairro Alto

Short trips should prioritize central neighborhoods and limit travel time between attractions.
