Lisbon is a strong destination for business travel, with good infrastructure and accessibility.

Best areas to stay:
- Avenida da Liberdade: central, upscale, business-friendly hotels
- Baixa: walkable and convenient
- Parque das Nações: modern area near conference centers

Transportation:
- Metro is efficient and connects key areas
- Taxis and ride-sharing are widely available

Tips:
- Plan extra time for travel due to hills and traffic
- Choose centrally located hotels to reduce commute time
- Many hotels offer business amenities and reliable Wi-Fi
