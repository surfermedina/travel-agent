Lisbon is known for its food scene, especially seafood and traditional Portuguese cuisine.

Key foods:
- Pastéis de nata (custard tarts)
- Bacalhau (salt cod dishes)
- Grilled sardines
- Seafood rice

Popular areas for food:
- Time Out Market (variety of vendors)
- Bairro Alto (restaurants and nightlife)
- Cais do Sodré (trendy dining)

Dining is generally relaxed, and meals can last longer than in the US. Reservations are recommended for popular spots.
