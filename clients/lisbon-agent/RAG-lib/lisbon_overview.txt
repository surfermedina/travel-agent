Lisbon is the capital of Portugal and one of Europe’s most scenic cities, known for its hills, historic neighborhoods, and coastal views.

Key areas:
- Alfama: the oldest district, known for narrow streets and traditional Fado music
- Baixa: central, flat, and ideal for first-time visitors
- Chiado: cultural area with shops, cafes, and restaurants
- Belém: home to major landmarks like Jerónimos Monastery and Belém Tower

Lisbon is compact but hilly, making it walkable but physically demanding. Public transportation includes trams, metro, and buses.

The city is known for viewpoints (miradouros), seafood, pastries like pastéis de nata, and a relaxed pace of life.
